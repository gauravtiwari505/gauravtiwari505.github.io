01Where you are

Where is your data today?

02What I do

Four ways to work together, from a short diagnostic to ongoing leadership.

  1. 01

    Data diagnostic and roadmap

    2–4 weeks

    What data exists, what is broken, and what to build first.

    I map your sources, systems and reports, find where the numbers stop agreeing, and give you a prioritised plan for what to build first.

  2. 02

    Modern data stack build or migration

    6–12 weeks

    A warehouse, dbt models and a BI layer your team can work in.

    Hands-on build or migration: ingestion into the warehouse, a tested dbt project (staging, intermediate, marts), and reporting in Looker or Metabase.

  3. 03

    Fractional Head of Data

    1–2 days a week

    Data leadership before you are ready to hire it full-time.

    Strategy and roadmap, advising the CEO or CPO, and hiring your first data people, with someone senior who can still write the models.

  4. 04

    Safe AI in the data workflow

    Scoped to your team

    Use AI on your pipelines without losing trust in the numbers.

    CI/CD for data, quality gates and verification for AI-assisted pipeline changes, so every change is checked against thresholds and leaves an audit trail.

Safe AI in the data workflow comes from my day job: at Spotify I lead the verification of AI-generated data pipeline changes, with quality gates, thresholds and an audit trail.

03How it works

Built to end with someone else owning the work.

  1. i

    Diagnose

    Talk to leadership and the people who use the data. Map what exists, where it lives, and where the numbers stop agreeing.

    You leave withA clear picture of your data and a prioritised roadmap.

  2. ii

    Build the foundation

    Hands-on: warehouse, tested dbt models and a BI layer, with quality checks in the delivery pipeline from the start.

    You leave withA platform your team can extend, not one only I understand.

  3. iii

    Set up the leadership

    Shape the data function and its roadmap with the CEO or CPO, and help hire the people who will own it.

    You leave withA team that owns the work after I step back.

Not every company needs all three. A diagnostic can stand on its own, or you can bring me in for just the build or just the leadership.
