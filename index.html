02What I do

Four ways to get your data ready for AI, from a short diagnostic to ongoing leadership.

  1. 01

    AI-readiness diagnostic

    2–4 weeks

    Can your data support AI agents, and what has to change first?

    I map your sources, definitions and reports, find where the numbers stop agreeing and what an agent would get wrong, and give you a prioritised plan for what to fix first.

  2. 02

    Safe AI in the data workflow

    Scoped to your team

    Let AI change your pipelines without losing trust in the numbers.

    CI/CD for data, quality gates and verification for AI-assisted pipeline changes, so every change is checked against thresholds and leaves an audit trail.

  3. 03

    Agent-ready data foundation

    6–12 weeks

    One set of trusted definitions for your people and your AI tools.

    Hands-on build or migration: a warehouse, a tested dbt project (staging, intermediate, marts), a semantic or metrics layer, and reporting in Looker or Metabase.

  4. 04

    Fractional Head of Data & AI

    1–2 days a week

    Data and AI leadership before you are ready to hire it full-time.

    Strategy and roadmap, advising the CEO or CPO on where AI fits, and hiring your first data people, with someone senior who can still write the models.

The AI work comes from my day job: at Spotify I lead the verification of AI-generated changes, including data pipeline changes, with quality gates, thresholds and an audit trail.

    Hands-on: warehouse, tested dbt models and a BI layer, with quality checks in the delivery pipeline from the start.

Not every company needs all three. A diagnostic can stand on its own, or you can bring me in for just the build or just the leadership.
